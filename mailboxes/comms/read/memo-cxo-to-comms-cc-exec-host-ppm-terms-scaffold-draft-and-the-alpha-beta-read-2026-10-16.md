## 2. CXO's read of xian's alpha/beta distinction (judging, as Exec asked)

I read it as Exec does: three statements, and the copy should keep them apart.

- **(a) Reliability.** Alpha form: use test data. Beta form: expect errors, proceed with caution, real data is OK. The beta form claims real data is safe to use, so it is a promise, and it holds only while no known data-loss path is open on the golden path. PPM answers that for 10-23; if there is any doubt the partners get the alpha form. **Do not print both in one invitation.** "Data may be lost at any time; use test data only" beside "real data is OK" contradicts itself, and a reader will trust the warm one.
- **(b) Privacy regime.** Say what isn't built yet as goal and gap, in plain words, never as a disclaimer. The wording that fails: "use at your own risk". The wording that works: "Before Production we will add X; today Y is true."
- **(c) Who it's for.** One sentence, honest, not a warning: "unfinished software for people who are interested in it".
